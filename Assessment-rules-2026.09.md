### TRADITIONAL-ESSAY  
| Grade | Mark | Remark | Descriptive equivalent |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| A+ | 80+ | Outstanding | Below plus considerable amount of critical judgement and originality |
| A | 70-79 | Excellent | Below plus some critical judgement and originality |
| A-, B+ | 65-69, 60-65 | Very Good | Below plus comprehensive command of the literature, its theories, and empirical findings |
| B, B- | 55-59, 50-54 | Good | Below plus good knowledge of the literature. Some analytical sophistication. Ability to combine factual knowledge with logical argument. Knowledge of the central concepts relevant to the question. |
| C+, C | 45-49, 40-44 | Adequate | Mostly coherent and relevant answer, showing basic knowledge of lectures and readings. Limited critical reflection on the readings and lectures. Some understanding of central concepts. Uses evidence reasonably well.  Might show some factual errors. |
| Fail | 20-39 | Unsatisfactory | Less than the above but some understanding of the subject. Incomplete answer based on poorly-understood lecture material. Unstructured. Numerous errors of fact. Concepts disordered or flawed. |
| Bad Fail | 0-19 | Very unsatisfactory | Less than the above. Shallow. Little evidence of understanding. |
