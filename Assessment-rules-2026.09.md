### POLICY-MEMO-ANALYTIC
| Grade    | Mark         | Remark              | Descriptive equivalent                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             |     |
| :------- | :----------- | :------------------ | :----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --- |
| A+       | 80+          | Outstanding         | Below plus exceptional policy judgement. Sees important trade-offs, constraints, risks, or consequences that are not obvious. Recommendation is unusually insightful, feasible, and compellingly supported.                                                        |     |
| A        | 70-79        | Excellent           | Below plus strong critical judgement in weighing alternatives. Explicit about relevant trade-offs, uncertainty, implementation, and institutional or political constraints. Clear and convincing recommendation follows from the analysis.                         |     |
| A-, B+   | 65-69, 60-64 | Very Good           | Defines the policy problem well. Uses appropriate evidence and analysis. Identifies credible policy alternatives and evaluates them against relevant criteria. Recommendation is specific, feasible, and substantially supported by the analysis.                  |     |
| B, B-    | 55-59, 50-54 | Good                | Understands the policy problem and provides competent analysis. Uses relevant evidence. Considers alternatives, although unevenly or incompletely. Recommendation is plausible but connections between evidence, trade-offs, and recommendation could be stronger. |     |
| C+, C    | 45-49, 40-44 | Adequate            | Identifies the broad policy problem and offers a relevant recommendation. Some useful evidence and analysis, but alternatives, trade-offs, feasibility, or implementation are treated superficially. May contain factual or analytical errors.                     |     |
| Fail     | 20-39        | Unsatisfactory      | Incomplete or poorly understood policy problem. Analysis is weak, evidence poorly used, or recommendation inadequately connected to the analysis. Important alternatives or constraints ignored.                                                                   |     |
| Bad Fail | 0-19         | Very unsatisfactory | Little evidence of understanding of the policy problem. Analysis does not provide a credible basis for policy choice. Recommendation absent, incoherent, or unsupported.                                                                                           |     |


### POLICY-MEMO-PROFESSIONAL
| Grade | Mark | Remark | Descriptive equivalent |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| A+ | 80+ | Outstanding | Below plus exceptional policy judgement. Anticipates what the decision-maker needs to know, including important risks, objections, constraints, or consequences that are not obvious. Could credibly inform a consequential real-world decision. |
| A | 70-79 | Excellent | Below plus strong judgement about what matters for the decision. Recommendation is compellingly supported, practically feasible, and attentive to trade-offs, stakeholders, implementation, and risk. Highly effective professional communication. |
| A-, B+ | 65-69, 60-64 | Very Good | Clearly identifies the decision to be made and what the decision-maker needs to know. Relevant evidence is selected and interpreted well. Recommendation is specific and actionable. Recognizes important practical constraints. Clear, concise, and well organized. |
| B, B- | 55-59, 50-54 | Good | Provides useful analysis of the decision and a plausible recommendation. Generally relevant evidence and clear writing, but analysis may contain unnecessary material or neglect some important constraints, trade-offs, stakeholders, or implementation issues. |
| C+, C | 45-49, 40-44 | Adequate | Broadly understands the policy issue and communicates a recognizable recommendation. Some relevant analysis, but insufficiently focused on the decision-maker's needs. Important evidence, alternatives, constraints, or practical implications may be missing. |
| Fail | 20-39 | Unsatisfactory | Limited understanding of the decision facing the recipient. Memo is poorly focused, inadequately supported, impractical, or difficult to use. Recommendation may be unclear or unsupported. |
| Bad Fail | 0-19 | Very unsatisfactory | Does not provide the recipient with a usable analysis of the policy decision. Little relevant evidence or reasoning. No credible actionable recommendation. |


### WHITE-PAPER-ANALYTIC
| Grade | Mark | Remark | Descriptive equivalent |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| A+ | 80+ | Outstanding | Below plus exceptional analytical and policy judgement. Develops an important insight not obvious from the existing evidence or debate. Integrates evidence, competing explanations, trade-offs, and constraints into an unusually compelling account of the policy problem and what should be done. |
| A | 70-79 | Excellent | Below plus strong critical judgement and synthesis. Evaluates competing explanations or approaches rather than merely reporting them. Handles evidence, uncertainty, trade-offs, and important counterarguments convincingly. Policy conclusions follow persuasively from the analysis. |
| A-, B+ | 65-69, 60-64 | Very Good | Defines and explains the policy problem clearly and comprehensively. Draws on a substantial and appropriate evidence base. Identifies important causal mechanisms, competing interpretations, and policy alternatives. Conclusions and recommendations are well supported by the analysis. |
| B, B- | 55-59, 50-54 | Good | Good understanding of the policy problem and relevant evidence. Analysis goes beyond description and identifies important causes, consequences, or policy alternatives. Some analytical sophistication, but synthesis, evaluation of evidence, alternatives, or connection to recommendations remains incomplete. |
| C+, C | 45-49, 40-44 | Adequate | Coherent and relevant account of the policy problem. Uses some appropriate evidence and demonstrates basic understanding of relevant concepts. Analysis is substantially descriptive, with limited evaluation of evidence, competing explanations, trade-offs, or policy alternatives. |
| Fail | 20-39 | Unsatisfactory | Incomplete or poorly understood account of the policy problem. Evidence is inadequate, poorly interpreted, or weakly connected to claims. Analysis lacks structure or contains significant factual or conceptual errors. Policy conclusions are inadequately supported. |
| Bad Fail | 0-19 | Very unsatisfactory | Little evidence of understanding of the policy problem. Shallow or seriously flawed analysis, with little credible evidence. No defensible policy conclusions. |


### WHITE-PAPER-PUBLIC-POLICY
| Grade | Mark | Remark | Descriptive equivalent |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| A+ | 80+ | Outstanding | Below plus exceptional policy judgement and intellectual contribution. Reframes or substantially clarifies an important policy problem and develops conclusions of genuine value to an informed policy audience. Could credibly influence how policymakers or specialists think about the issue. |
| A | 70-79 | Excellent | Below plus a distinctive and convincing policy argument. Synthesizes complex evidence into a clear account of what matters and why. Confronts serious objections, uncertainty, competing interests, and implementation constraints. Recommendations are consequential, realistic, and convincingly justified. |
| A-, B+ | 65-69, 60-64 | Very Good | Provides an authoritative, accessible account of the policy problem for an informed non-specialist audience. Selects and synthesizes relevant evidence well. Establishes clear policy implications and credible recommendations. Recognizes major trade-offs, stakeholders, and implementation constraints. |
| B, B- | 55-59, 50-54 | Good | Provides a useful and generally convincing account of the policy problem. Evidence is relevant and policy implications are identifiable. Recommendations are plausible, but the paper may be insufficiently selective, synthetic, distinctive, or attentive to implementation and competing interests. |
| C+, C | 45-49, 40-44 | Adequate | Provides a recognizable account of the issue and some relevant policy conclusions. Evidence and analysis are adequate but substantially descriptive. Limited synthesis, prioritization, consideration of alternatives, or attention to the needs of a policy audience. |
| Fail | 20-39 | Unsatisfactory | Does not provide a sufficiently reliable or useful account of the policy problem for an informed policy audience. Weak evidence, unclear argument, major omissions, impractical conclusions, or poor organization substantially reduce its usefulness. |
| Bad Fail | 0-19 | Very unsatisfactory | Would seriously misinform or fail to inform its intended policy audience. Little credible analysis or evidence and no defensible policy direction. |


### ESSAY-TRADITIONAL
| Grade | Mark | Remark | Descriptive equivalent |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| A+ | 80+ | Outstanding | Below plus considerable amount of critical judgement and originality |
| A | 70-79 | Excellent | Below plus some critical judgement and originality |
| A-, B+ | 65-69, 60-64 | Very Good | Below plus comprehensive command of the literature, its theories, and empirical findings |
| B, B- | 55-59, 50-54 | Good | Below plus good knowledge of the literature. Some analytical sophistication. Ability to combine factual knowledge with logical argument. Knowledge of the central concepts relevant to the question. |
| C+, C | 45-49, 40-44 | Adequate | Mostly coherent and relevant answer, showing basic knowledge of lectures and readings. Limited critical reflection on the readings and lectures. Some understanding of central concepts. Uses evidence reasonably well.  Might show some factual errors. |
| Fail | 20-39 | Unsatisfactory | Less than the above but some understanding of the subject. Incomplete answer based on poorly-understood lecture material. Unstructured. Numerous errors of fact. Concepts disordered or flawed. |
| Bad Fail | 0-19 | Very unsatisfactory | Less than the above. Shallow. Little evidence of understanding. |
