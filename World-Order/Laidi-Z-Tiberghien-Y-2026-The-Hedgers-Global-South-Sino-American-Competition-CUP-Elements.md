Laidi, Zaiki and Tiberghien, Yves. 2026.  *The Hedgers: How the Global South is Navigating Sino-US Competition*, Cambridge: Cambridge University Press Cambridge Elements  

For the last century, more than 70% of humanity has lived in states that are not frontline Great Power rivals.  Yet, even though the international relations of those states are researched, they are not traditionally thought to be central or formative for the big picture that is considered world order.  Instead, such states are imagined to merely respond to major power actions, admittedly in a bewildering variety of extremely creative ways.  For eight middle powers of the Global South, Laidi and Tiberghien study active hedging, i.e., strategies that more than passively respond to the currents of Great Power rivalry.  In doing this, the authors interrogate that subtle shifting margin between merely navigating and actually influencing world order.  The picture that emerges is rich and fascinating, and should disrupt conventional thinking on the international system.
