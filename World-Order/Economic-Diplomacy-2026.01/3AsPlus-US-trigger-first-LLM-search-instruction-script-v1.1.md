# Trigger-first search: US economic statecraft and exposed Asian Third Nations — Tier 1

## Controlling documents
Follow `3AsPlus-category-coding-v1.1.md`, `3AsPlus-sources-v1.0.md`, `Interim-Results-coding-v1.1.md`, **`3AsPlus-Tier1-outcome-screening-v1.0.md`**, and **`3AsPlus-US-trigger-output-format-v1.1.md`**. Both discovery routes must merge into the same stable-ID workbook. The output-format document controls worksheets and validation.

## Objective and scope
Independently enumerate identifiable dated US economic-statecraft actions from January 2000 to collection date in trade policy, technology controls, financial sanctions, and investment restrictions. Include documented restrictions, threats, conditional inducements and cooperative offers; exclude ordinary macroeconomic spillovers. Group action stages within `trigger_episode_id`. Identify **documented** exposure of ASEAN members, Japan, South Korea, India and Australia; do not presume all countries are exposed.

## Collection steps
1. Assemble dated US actions from USTR, White House, BIS, OFAC, Federal Register, Treasury Investment Security, USITC, State Department and relevant additional sources. Verify announcement versus legal effectiveness and source URLs.
2. Deduplicate trigger actions, link stages into episodes, and assign stable IDs. Record trigger magnitude, target and source evidence.
3. For each documented trigger-jurisdiction exposure, establish mechanism and confidence, then run a predeclared symmetric response search battery with neutral and category-specific terms, relevant languages and official/independent sources. Log zero yields and limitations.
4. Record `response_found=NO` **only** after the complete exposure-cell search battery finds no eligible response. Otherwise use `UNDETERMINED` where no qualifying response has yet been established. An ASEAN bloc response is not ten national responses.
5. Verify qualifying responses under the category protocol, link them through Trigger-Event Links, and reuse existing Events IDs when rediscovered. A weak/contextual link alone does not establish positive trigger-conditioned incidence.
6. For each accepted response event, apply **Tier 1 bounded outcome screening**, not mandatory exhaustive 60-day reconstruction. Reuse already documented US dispositions and shared settlements; code positive movement only with affirmative evidence, otherwise uncertainty.
7. Save validated, bounded checkpoints including search effort, source reuse, rejected candidates, missingness and full coverage status. Distinguish completed search attempts from completed exposure-cell searches.

## Batching and stopping
A trigger-first census is not complete upon finding a target number of response events. Define bounded batches in advance (e.g. year × modality), report actions, episodes, exposure cells, searched cells, positive-response cells and no-response-found cells. On work limits, stop with outstanding cells marked PARTIAL or NOT_STARTED. Never present the response-side trigger subset as a completed trigger census.

## Output and interpretation
Merge into the ten-tab workbook prescribed by `3AsPlus-US-trigger-output-format-v1.1.md`. Retain Tier 1 ascertainment fields and measured resource logs. Full response strategy and episode histories support Propositions 1 and 2, subject to coverage. Outcome summaries for Proposition 3 are **among documented dispositions**, not a representative complete 60-day panel; reserve full outcome comparisons for the Tier 2 stratified sample.
