# Response-first search: Asian economic diplomacy responding to US economic statecraft — Tier 1

## Controlling documents
Follow `3AsPlus-category-coding-v1.2.md`, `3AsPlus-sources-v1.1.md`, `Interim-Results-coding-v1.2.md`, **`3AsPlus-Tier1-outcome-screening-v1.1.md`**, and **`3AsPlus-US-trigger-output-format-v1.2.md`**. The last controls workbook layout and validation. The Tier 1 addendum controls routine follow-up *effort*, not the definitions of outcomes. Never silently use the older output specification.

## Objective and scope
Discover dated, sourced actions by governments, regulators, authorised industry bodies and regional blocs in ASEAN member states, Japan, South Korea, India and Australia responding to US economic statecraft, from January 2000 to the collection date. Work year-by-year and country-by-country, including 2000–2016. Search all four US modalities: trade policy, technology controls, financial sanctions, and industrial policy (including subsidies, tax credits, domestic-content conditions, procurement preferences, investment incentives, and inbound/outbound investment restrictions). Include restriction, threat, conditional inducement, and cooperative offer. Use category-neutral as well as Acquiescence, Adaptation, Aggregation, Resistance and OTHER searches; use local-language material when feasible. Do not invent events, motives, links, dates or quotations.

## Collection steps
1. Search source families in `3AsPlus-sources-v1.1.md` and relevant independent reporting. Log exact queries and zero yields; normalize originating source family rather than syndication host.
2. Verify the dated Third-Nation action, actor, instrument, documentary evidence, stage, and US connection. Apply category protocol and non-exclusive coding; weak/unestablished links remain candidates, not confirmed trigger-conditioned responses.
3. Deduplicate by substantive underlying policy action and stable IDs; link distinct policy-package actions without multiplying a single bargain. Record regional-bloc actions once, not once per member.
4. Create/merge Events, US Triggers, Trigger-Event Links, and documented Trigger Exposure cells. Preserve multiple supported trigger links. For exposure cells not systematically searched, record `NOT_STARTED` or `PARTIAL` and `UNDETERMINED`, never `NO`.
5. Apply **Tier 1 bounded outcome screening to every accepted event**. Populate the Interim Results record with positive evidence, search-depth fields, and uncertainty. Do **not** undertake mandatory exhaustive 60-day follow-up. Retain day-60 window for comparability, flag elapsed vs incomplete window separately from research completeness.
6. Reuse already verified source documents and shared settlements. Validate IDs, dates, formulas, categories, outcome status, and source URLs after each checkpoint. Save partial batches with audit trail and measured resource indicators.

## Stopping and batching
The current scripts specify a *long-run ceiling/target* of **100000 qualifying response events**, or exhaustion of clear leads; preserve that value rather than reverting without authorisation. **It is not a single-run quota.** Each execution must declare a bounded `batch_id`, years, jurisdictions, modalities, and a maximum number of candidates/events or work budget *before searching*. If no batch scope is supplied, use a conservative pilot batch and report the chosen scope; do not attempt 100000 events in one run. Stop at the batch boundary, document incomplete coverage, and never pad counts.

## Output
Use the ten-tab integrated workbook exactly as `3AsPlus-US-trigger-output-format-v1.2.md` specifies, with new Tier 1 outcome and measurement fields. Report new/updated unique events, triggers, exposure cells, links and settlements; source concentration; unresolved and positively documented outcomes; search coverage; measured effort and unavailable metrics. Propositions 1 and 2 may use the full validated response data. Proposition 3 is **descriptive context only** pending the Tier 2 stratified investigation. No causal conclusions.

## Current four-modality search vocabulary

The fourth modality is `INDUSTRIAL_POLICY`, including subsidies, tax credits, domestic-content and final-assembly rules, procurement preferences, industrial investment incentives, inbound investment screening and outbound investment restrictions **when documented as external economic statecraft**. Use `trigger_instrument` to preserve the particular instrument. Search for Korea's response to IRA EV credits and Japan's differentiated 2023 semiconductor alignment as instructive leads, applying ordinary independent verification rather than treating these examples as automatically accepted records. Do not create obsolete top-level investment-restrictions fields.
