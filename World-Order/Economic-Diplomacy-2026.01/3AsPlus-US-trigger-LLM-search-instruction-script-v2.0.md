# Response-first search: Asian economic diplomacy responding to US economic statecraft

## Controlling documents
Read and follow `3AsPlus-category-coding-v1.1.md`, `Interim-Results-coding-v1.1.md`, `3AsPlus-sources-v1.0.md`, and `3AsPlus-US-trigger-output-format-v1.0.md`. The last file is the sole controlling workbook/output specification: do not improvise tabs, field names, denominators, or missing-data codes. Preserve and merge into an existing integrated workbook by stable IDs, not by appending duplicate observations.

## Search objective and scope
Find dated, sourced actions by governments, regulators, authorised industry bodies, and regional blocs in ASEAN member states, Japan, South Korea, India, and Australia responding to US economic statecraft, from **January 2000 to the collection date**. Start with Asian responses and trace backward to named US actions and forward through the 60-day interim window. Do not invent events, sources, dates, quotes, or causal links. Distinguish response-side discovery from a complete trigger-first census.

Four US trigger modalities: (1) trade policy (tariffs, Section 301/232, safeguards, GSP, market-access/currency-related trade demands); (2) technology controls (BIS, Entity List, semiconductor equipment, licensing); (3) financial sanctions (OFAC, secondary sanctions, financial-transaction restrictions); (4) investment restrictions (CFIUS-related measures, outbound-investment restrictions, investment screening). A measure can have multiple modality indicators; use the primary modality as specified in the shared output format. Distinguish coercion, threats, conditional inducements, and cooperative offers.

## Search method
Search year-by-year and country-by-country, including **2000-2016** rather than treating it as backfill. Search across the four trigger modalities and all five response categories, including Resistance and OTHER; search both category signal language and neutral response terms to avoid finding only obvious 3As. Use multiple independent queries and sources, including local-language official materials where feasible. Consult the sources in `3AsPlus-sources-v1.0.md` at minimum, plus appropriate independent reporting and relevant BIS, OFAC, Federal Register, Treasury Investment Security, and European Commission sources; record significant additional source families.

For each candidate, verify the date, actor, substantive action, source URL, strategy evidence, US trigger connection, policy-package/stage boundaries, and source family. Record weak or unestablished trigger connections as candidates rather than confirmed linked responses. Do not count multiple news articles as multiple actions. Do not automatically multiply a bloc statement across member states. Record the searches and their zero yields in Search Log.

For accepted events, create/merge Events, US Triggers, Trigger-Event Links, and Interim Results records. Populate Trigger Exposure only where exposure is independently documented; mark unsurveyed exposure cells `NOT_STARTED` or `PARTIAL`, never `NO`. Search the full 60-day interim window when elapsed; otherwise mark `WINDOW_INCOMPLETE`. Capture contemporaneous evidence for each side's movement; do not infer causal effects from succession or a joint bargain. Apply the same ID conventions as the trigger-first script.

## Stopping and reporting
Unless instructed otherwise, stop after approximately **90-100 distinct qualifying response events**, or earlier when clear leads are exhausted; do not pad the sample. This is an event-count target, not a target for articles, triggers, packages, or settlements. Do not claim a completed US-trigger census. Report search coverage, incomplete windows, source-family concentration, weak links, and unresolved coding cases. Output exactly as prescribed by `3AsPlus-US-trigger-output-format-v1.0.md`.
