following set of tables, figures, and episode studies (this last each about a single A4-page description with an accompanying timeline):  

### Four Tables

|Exhibit|Purpose|
|---|---|
|**Table 1. The strategic repertoire**|Frequency and distribution of the four modalities across observed response events, policies, and packages|
|**Table 2. Conditional strategic responses**|Four strategies by US instrument, trigger mode, and exposure type|
|**Table 3. Strategic portfolios**|Co-occurrence and conditional probabilities of strategies within country–episodes|
|**Table 4. Strategic sequencing and interaction**|Transition probabilities, timing relationships, and subsequent US dispositions|

### Three Figures

**Figure 1. The evolution of Asian economic diplomacy**

Annual frequencies and shares of each modality.

**Figure 2. From immediate response to cumulative strategy**

A transition diagram showing the distribution of initial strategies and the subsequent additions of other modalities.

This will likely be best represented as a Sankey diagram in R for the final empirical presentation.

**Figure 3. The evolving interaction**

A chronology that combines dated American policy stages with Asian response modalities, either for a selected major episode or for a comparative set of country–episodes.

The figure should emphasise sequence rather than imply causality.

### Four Episode Studies

Where possible select cases illustrating:

1. Acquiescence followed by Adaptation.
2. Acquiescence followed by Aggregation.
3. Resistance followed by Acquiescence (or whatever can be interpreted as accommodation), or vice versa.
4. Different Asian strategies in response to the same American policy episode.
